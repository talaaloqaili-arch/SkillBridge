How It Works

Three simple steps. A two-way win.

No one-way lectures. Just students helping students move forward.

01

Share the skills you know

Tell the community what you can teach and what you would love to learn. Small skills count, too.

I can teach Python. I want to learn Figma.
02

Find a complementary peer

Explore skills and connect with a student whose strengths match your learning goals.

They know Figma. They want to learn Python.
03

Exchange knowledge together

Agree on a goal, choose a time, and take turns teaching. Put what you learn into practice.

Two students. Two skills. Shared progress.
